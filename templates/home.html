How MediPulse AI Works

MediPulse AI combines Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, Cloud Computing, and Smart Healthcare services to provide fast, secure, and intelligent healthcare assistance.

01

Login Securely

Sign in using your Google account to securely access all AI healthcare services and personalized features.

02

Select a Service

Choose from AI Health Assistant, Medicine Finder, Prescription Scanner, Blood Donation, AI Eye Scanner, Emergency Navigation, and more.

03

AI Processes Your Request

OpenRouter AI and Gemini Vision analyze your request or uploaded image and generate intelligent healthcare insights instantly.

04

Get Instant Results

Receive medicine details, hospital locations, AI analysis, donor matches, reminders, or healthcare recommendations within seconds.

05

Cloud Storage

Your reminders, blood donation records, complaints, and profile data are securely stored in the cloud using Supabase.

06

Smart Automation

MediPulse automatically sends medicine reminders, blood donor notifications, and performs scheduled AI healthcare tasks.
